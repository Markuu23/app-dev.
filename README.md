My Favorite Series

## Prison Break

**Prison Break** is my favorite TV series. It is about a man named Michael Scofield who tries to help his brother escape from prison.

### Why I like it:
- Exciting and full of suspense  
- Smart and creative plans  
- Interesting characters  

### Main Characters:
- Michael Scofield  
- Lincoln Burrows  
- Sara Tancredi
